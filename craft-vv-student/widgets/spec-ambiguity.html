The golden model code/lib/models/alu_model.py and code/dut/alu.sv pick the signed reading of overflow and the borrow reading of carry (sub_ext[WIDTH] — the model's own comment says # borrow). Nothing in the quoted sentences forces either choice; the vplan row is what pins it. The same split, in silicon and in the demo. Borrow vs no-borrow both ship in production instruction sets — x86 sets the flag on borrow, ARM clears it. The Session 1 demo (just demo 02 s1 0) builds both overflow readings as RTL (alu_ovf_signed.sv vs alu_ovf_carry.sv): one test, both pass; the spec's own corner list then splits them on 118 of 272 vectors, first witness 0x7F + 0x01.
