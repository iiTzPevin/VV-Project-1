What this models. Exactly the gating in dut/fifo.sv, depth 8: do_wr = wr_en & ~full, do_rd = rd_en & ~empty, full = (count == 8), empty = (count == 0); a simultaneous accepted write and read leaves count unchanged. One sample per rising edge. a |=> b is evaluated as a |-> ##1 b; an attempt whose obligation lands past the last cycle is left unfinished at end of sim — reported, not failed. The ignore_full trace is the released mutant: do_wr = wr_en, so a write while full lands anyway and overwrites in place.

Not modeled: multi-cycle sequences, $past, disable iff — the class suite (dut/fifo_sva.sv) uses all three, and the vacuity trap is identical there. For the live version of this trap: just demo 07 s1 2 — an all-green suite on RTL that drops writes on the floor, exposed by two covers at zero. HW7's standing rule follows: every assert ships with a passing cover, and a dead antecedent earns nothing.
