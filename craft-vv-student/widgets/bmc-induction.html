What Act 1 models. A 10-state trigger FSM: the right nibble advances, the wrong one restarts (or credits state 1 if it matches the first key nibble), and state 9 latches. BMC here is exact breadth-first search over those states; a SAT engine asks the same bounded question over the 16k input sequences and answers identically. The class trojan (just demo 08 s2 1, dut/arbiter_trojan.sv) triggers on one combinational pattern and falls at step 1 — this one is nine deep, so the depth dial has something to say. Expected-first-hit for the random walk is solved from the chain's linear system, not sampled.

What Act 2 models. The registers of dut/fifo.sv that decide count and the flags: the 4-bit count as declared, two 3-bit pointers, the shipped gating (do_wr = wr_en & ~full, full = count==8). The memory array and data are projected away — they cannot affect these three. The induction step is the honest quantifier: search over windows of k pairwise-distinct states satisfying the asserted properties (the simple-path constraint sby uses) for one more transition that violates. At 1,024 states, exhaustive search answers exactly what smtbmc asks z3. This is the demo's Act: just demo 08 s1 2 (true bound, induction refuses) and just demo 08 s1 3 (one added assert, successful proof by k-induction).
