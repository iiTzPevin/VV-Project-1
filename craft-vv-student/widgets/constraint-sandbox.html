Occupancy over time

Reading the drift. Drift is measured separately for the fill and drain phases — entries added per cycle. Averaging the two would cancel to zero for any working sawtooth, which is why the demo measures per phase too. Near-zero on both means the constraints are shaping nothing, whatever the histogram happens to show.

What this models. One clock per cycle, exactly the gating in code/dut/fifo.sv: a write is taken only when not full, a read only when not empty, and a simultaneous read+write leaves the count unchanged. Depth 8. Writes while full counts the cycles where wr_en was asserted with full high — the only cycles on which the released mutant ignore_full can be detected.

This reproduces the phenomenon, not the exact stream from pyvsc — the numbers will be close to the Session 2 demo, not identical. For the real thing: just demo 05 s2 4.
